1. Convert rwxrwxrwx, rwxr-xr-x, rw-r--r--, rw-------, r-xr-xr-x to numeric values.

a. rwxrwxrwx --777
b.rwxr-xr-x --755
c.rw-r--r-- --644
d.rw------- --600
e.r-xr-xr-x -- 555

Explain why 777 is dangerous and why 600 is recommended.

In Linux, 777 is dangerous because everyone can read, modify, and execute a file, while 600 restricts access to the owner only.
